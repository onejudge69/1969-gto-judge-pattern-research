Open Questions: What Is Still Unresolved

These questions are open. Each is labeled under Policy v1.0 and says what record would answer it. Have a record that would settle one of them? Submit your documents.

1. Build order of the DON 00017 cars across plants Unresolved

What is known: Five Carousel Red Judges print dealer order number 00017: Framingham, Fremont, Arlington and two at Atlanta. The Framingham car has the earliest invoice date, 01/30/69, and the earliest cowl week, 01B, of the five on file. Documented fact for the dates. Invoice dates are billing dates, and cowl weeks from different plants do not show the order the cars were built across plants. Corroborated interpretation Addendum 2026-10-20: DON 00017 was one car per zone, not a five-car “corporate pilot batch, one per plant.” Documented fact Al’s ruling, Oct 20, 2026. Basis: the five DON 00017 cars on file came from different plants and went to different zones.

What record would settle it: A plant build-sequence or production-control record, or a PMD record ordering these cars by build date. Not documented in primary sources currently available.

2. Which Judge was built first Unresolved

What is known: No document on file identifies any single car as the first Judge. This site does not make that claim. “Lead unit” on this site means earliest invoice date and cowl week among the DON 00017 cars only. Addendum 2026-10-20: For Framingham only: VIN 242379G125xxx is the first Framingham Judge. Documented fact Al’s ruling, Oct 20, 2026. Basis: PHS records; Mattison’s email of Nov 26, 2018, in which he called it his “guess”; the earliest invoice date, 01/30/69, and the earliest cowl week, 01B, of the five known DON 00017 cars. Which Judge was built first at any plant stays open.

What record would settle it: A dated Pontiac or Fisher Body record naming a VIN as the first Judge built. Not documented in primary sources currently available.

3. What ship-to 992 means Unresolved

What is known: PHS invoices print ship-to codes such as 12 992. Documented fact For 29-991 a freight waybill for another car shows the Zone 29 facility in South Kearny, N.J. No shipping record for any 992 car is on file, so what 992 marks, and how it differs from 991, is a Hypothesis

What record would settle it: A waybill, shipping record or zone paper for a car invoiced to a 992 ship-to.

4. Remaining gaps on the Atlanta and Arlington DON 00017 cars Unresolved

What is known: PHS invoices for VINs 242379A119165, 242379A120714 and 242379R130631 are on file; the A119165 and R130631 copies print DON 00017. Documented fact Tier 2 See the DON 00017 page. Cowl tags matched to the Atlanta VINs rest on Al’s declaration. Corroborated interpretation Photos that show a cowl tag and VIN on the same car, and a clearer DON field on the A120714 invoice, are still wanted.

What record would settle the remaining gaps: Same-car photos of tag and VIN, and a sharper A120714 invoice DON field.

5. Whether the Framingham car, VIN 242379G125xxx, was at any drive-away or event Unresolved

What is known: The Boston Herald Traveler of Feb 2, 1969 reports 25 Carousel Red hardtops assigned to dealers to be driven to their dealerships. Documented fact as to what the article reports. The article names no VIN, and no record ties this car to that or any other event. Not documented in primary sources currently available.

What record would settle it: A dated photo, dealer paper or zone record naming the car's VIN at an event.

6. Zone-office hold details Unresolved

What is known: Ship-to 29-991 is a zone-held destination, consigned to the zone, not a dealer. Documented fact Whether cars were held there for a set period, why, and for how long: Hypothesis. No document states a hold.

What record would settle it: Zone 29 office records, release orders or dealer messages giving hold or release dates. Not documented in primary sources currently available.

7. Page 2 of the May 8, 1969 Goad memo Unresolved

What is known: Page 1 states production “started in mid January with 1,000 pattern cars being built in Carousel Red only.” Documented fact Page 2 is not on file.

What record would settle it: A copy of page 2 from the holder of the original. A request is outstanding.

8. Ownership of the Framingham car after March 1969 Unresolved

What is known: The car was sold through Allen Pontiac, Belleville, N.J. Documented fact Who owned it between then and later owners is not documented. Ownership history is kept off this site.

What record would settle it: Original title, registration or dealer records for the years after 1969. Not documented in primary sources currently available.

9. Lead, addendum of 2026-10-20: Feb 24, 1969 dash nameplate bulletin Unresolved

What is known: Tier 4 lead, pending an original. A web copy on the Wallace Racing site of PMD Dealer Service Information Bulletin 69-I-49, Section 2, dated 2-24-69, “Production Deletions on 1969 Tempest Models,” reads: “This bulletin is to inform you that the following items have been deleted in production and not merely left off. These items are not to be installed on vehicles not so equipped.” The list includes “Instrument Panel Name Plates - All except ‘Judge’”, plus the hood insulator pad, 6 cylinder engines only; lower rear quarter molding, all; parking brake pedal trim, all. Corroborated interpretation pending an original: by Feb 24, 1969 the Judge was the only Tempest-line car keeping the dash nameplate. The bulletin says nothing about the first car or build order.

What record would settle it: An original or PMD-archive copy of Dealer Service Information Bulletin 69-I-49. Not documented in primary sources currently available.
