Submit Your 1969 GTO Judge Documents

If you own a 1969 GTO Judge, or hold its papers, your documents can help fill gaps in the record. Early January–February 1969 cars and pattern cars are especially needed: Carousel Red cars, low dealer order numbers (000xx), and ship-to codes ending in 991, 992, 993 or 995.

What to send

ItemHow it is used
The full PHS invoice, every page, uncroppedTier 2 traceable copy. It shows the plant, dealer order number, ship-to, dealer, dates and code 554. Cropped or edited images carry less weight.
Cowl tag (data plate) photo, clear and straight-onTier 1 when photographed on the car. It shows the build week, trim, paint and body number.
VIN tag photoTier 1. It ties the other papers to the car.
Broadcast (build) sheetTier 1. It shows the order, the charge-to and ship-to, and WT1.
Dealer papers: original bill of sale, window sticker, order form, shipping papersTier 1 if original. They show where the car first went.
Stories, registry entries, listingsTier 4 leads only. Welcome, but they are not treated as facts.

The same VIN readable on the invoice, the broadcast sheet and the original sale paper makes the strongest case.

Privacy

  • Owner names and full VINs stay private unless the owner agrees in writing to publish them.
  • No car is scored, rated or valued. No owner, seller or researcher is accused of anything.
  • Documents are described exactly as printed. Conflicts are shown, not settled by guesswork.
  • The researcher's own lead-unit car is shown publicly as VIN 242379G125xxx. Its full VIN is shared privately with owners who submit documentation for their own car.
  • You can ask at any time for your material to be removed from the research.
