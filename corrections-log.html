Oct 15, 2026Broadcast sheet order-number box (Framingham car)
The earlier reading was changed after the owner inspected the original sheet in person: the box reads 000017, matching DON 00017 on the PHS invoice. A close-up photo is still to be added.
Oct 15, 2026Invoice and body numbers
ZGA 453185 is the invoice number, not a body sequence. FN 56607 is the Fisher body number, not the trim. The Fremont invoice number reads ZZA 468236 (an older summary printed 468216); the invoice governs.
Oct 15, 2026Unsupported items removed
An “unbroken title trail”, “Pattern Judge #22”, an engine-noise freeze, a 6,833 total, pool names and an embargo are not documented in primary sources currently available. Wrong VINs in an older audit were replaced by the VINs printed on the invoices.
Oct 15, 2026Oct 31, 1968 PMD release
First listed as a lead only; a copy was later filed. It describes an unnamed orange 350 H.O. car, not the Judge as announced.
Oct 17, 202629-991 ruling
29-991 is the Pontiac Zone 29 holding facility, Navy Industrial Yard, Building 2, South Kearny, N.J. (Anchor waybill for another car). It is not the Boston zone (Boston is Zone 01).
Oct 17, 2026No Baltimore car in DON 00017
No Baltimore car prints DON 00017 on the paper on file. The nearest Baltimore order in the material is DON 00018.
Oct 17, 2026Arlington cowl tag filed separately
The Arlington R130xxx cowl tag set is filed separately from the Fremont Z114xxx car; it does not belong to the Fremont car.
Oct 19, 2026Earlier forum wording withdrawn
The words “earliest confirmed production Judge”, “locked” and “validated” used in earlier forum posts are withdrawn. No document identifies the first Judge built.
Oct 20, 2026Three rulings by Al Diaz (Addendum 2026-10-20)
(A) VIN 242379G125xxx is the first Framingham Judge. Documented fact (Al’s ruling); Al Diaz ruling, Oct 20, 2026; basis: the five DON 00017 cars on file came from different plants and went to different zones. Documented fact (Al’s ruling); Al Diaz ruling, Oct 20, 2026; basis: the owner’s reading of the original sheet (see Oct 15 entry), and the same reading of the top line (“186276 - 000017”) by a forum poster in Nov 2025 (Tier 4 lead).
