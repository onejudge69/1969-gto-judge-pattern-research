1969 GTO Judge Release Timeline from Pontiac Documents

Each entry quotes the document exactly. None of these documents names a VIN, dealer order number or dealer for a specific car.

Sep 26, 1968
Car Distribution Bulletin 69-21, J. T. Wilson, Director of Car Distribution“a special GTO model named the “Judge”. Due to late engineering changes, this model will not go into production until approximately January 1, 1969.”
Tier 2 Documented fact
Oct 31, 1968
PMD Public Relations release“The first 5,000 units will be painted a special orange color and will have distinctive striping.” The car is yet to be named and has a 350 H.O. engine.
Tier 2 Documented fact That it describes a planned car, not the Judge as announced: Corroborated interpretation
Dec 11, 1968
Agency Conference Report No. 869, client Pontiac Motor Division“‘The Judge’ Announcement Promotion Package - SA 3091”
Tier 2 Documented fact
Undated
“THE JUDGE by Pontiac Quick Reference Specifications,” filed with Conf. Report 869“Test weight, pre-production car N.A.”
Tier 2 Documented fact Date: Unresolved
Dec 12, 1968
Car Distribution Bulletin 69-48, J. T. Wilson“production to start late January at all ‘A’ Assembly Plants”; “Color Carousel Red with Trim 257 Parchment or Trim 258 Black.”
Tier 2 Documented fact
Dec 19, 1968
PMD release, Examiner reference library copy stamped Dec 24, 1968“The first production units will be painted a bright red.”
Documented fact about the wording visible in the held copy. The page headed “HERE IT COMES” says Pontiac will introduce The Judge “next month” and lists a 366-horsepower V-8 Ram Air engine and a 60-inch floating deck air foil, as a paraphrase. The release line reads Thursday, December 19, 1968; the visible Examiner Reference Library receipt stamp reads Dec 24, 1968.
Copy provenance unresolved: this is the photographed copy held in Al Diaz’s research files, not an authenticated original. Its chain of custody and acquisition source are unconfirmed; attribution to Mike Noun is not established. The existing Tier 2 listing identifies the purported factory-record copy, but its uncertain origin limits evidentiary weight under the policy. The image wording does not identify a VIN, DON, zone allocation, first nationwide car, or actual production start date. See copy-provenance note.
Undated
PMD release, page 2 only“the first production units will be painted a bright red including the Endura energy-absorbing front bumper.”
Tier 2 Documented fact Date: Unresolved

F. J. Gordon letter on accessories and option codes; page “Revisions to Oct. 29th Listing”“554-WT1 NEW OPTION TEMPEST”
Tier 2 Documented fact That this page is the letter’s attachment rests on owner identification.

Tier 2 Documented fact
Jan 14, 1969
Car Distribution Bulletin No. 58, F. J. Gordon, rear antenna“NOT AVAILABLE ON G.T.O. WHEN THE JUDGE OPTION CODE 554 UPC CODE WT1 IS ORDERED.”
Tier 2 Documented fact
Late Jan 1969, printed Feb 2, 1969
Boston Herald Traveler, “Pontiac ‘Judge’ is Here,” John Rooney“Twenty five of the new Carousel Red, hardtop coupes were assigned to dealers to be driven to their dealerships.”
Tier 3 Documented fact What the article reports; it names no VIN.
Feb 17, 1969
The Pontiac Press, Pontiac, Mich., page C-6: retail ad“The Judge.. Now presiding at Pontiac Retail Store, 65 University Drive and E. Wide Track.” The ad lists a 366-hp 400 Ram Air V-8, floor-mounted 3-speed, 60-inch spoiler, mag-type wheels and “blue-red-yellow striping.”
Tier 3 Documented fact Period newspaper.
