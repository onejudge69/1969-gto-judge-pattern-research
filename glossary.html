Glossary of Terms

Short, plain definitions of terms used on this site.

Car and paperwork terms

VIN

Vehicle Identification Number. On a 1969 GTO it reads like 242379G1xxxxx: 2 = Pontiac, 42 = GTO series, 37 = two-door hardtop, 9 = 1969, the letter = assembly plant, then the unit number.

DON (dealer order number)

The order number printed on the factory invoice. Early Judges often show low numbers such as 00017. A DON shows an order, not build position.

PHS (Pontiac Historic Services)

The company holding Pontiac's factory invoice and billing records. It supplies copies of a car's original invoice.

Broadcast sheet

A plant paper that traveled with the car down the line, listing its options and order data. Also called a build sheet.

Cowl tag / trim tag

The Fisher Body data plate on the firewall. It shows the build week code, body style, plant and body number, trim and paint.

Body number

The Fisher Body serial number on the cowl tag (for example a prefix plus digits). It is not the VIN and not the invoice number.

Build week code (e.g. 01B)

On the cowl tag: the month (01 = January) and the week of that month (A, B, C, D, E). 01B means the second week of January 1969.

WT1

The UPC (option) code for The Judge package.

Sales code 554

The sales option code for The Judge. Pontiac listed it as “554-WT1 NEW OPTION TEMPEST.” The Judge was an option on the GTO, not a separate model.

Ship-to code

The destination code on the invoice: a zone number and a dealer or facility number, for example 29 991.

Zone

A Pontiac regional sales and distribution office, numbered (for example Zone 29).

29-991

The Pontiac Motor Division Zone 29 holding facility at Navy Industrial Yard, Building 2, South Kearny, N.J. Documented fact per an Anchor Motor Freight waybill for another car consigned to that code. This proves what 991 means for Zone 29 only. It is not the Boston zone.

Research terms

Pattern car

The May 8, 1969 Goad memo says production began with “1,000 pattern cars being built in Carousel Red only.” No Pontiac page on file lists which VINs were pattern cars.

Pilot batch

On this site, the group of five Judges that print the same DON 00017. Addendum 2026-10-20: Term withdrawn. DON 00017 was one car per zone, not a five-car “corporate pilot batch, one per plant.” Documented fact (Al's ruling). (Al Diaz ruling, Oct 20, 2026; basis: the five DON 00017 cars on file came from different plants and went to different zones.)

Lead unit

On this site: the car with the earliest invoice date and earliest cowl week among the DON 00017 cars on file. It does not mean the first Judge built. Addendum 2026-10-20: the lead unit, VIN 242379G125xxx, is the first Framingham Judge (Al Diaz ruling, Oct 20, 2026; Documented fact (Al’s ruling)).

Carousel Red

Pontiac paint code 72 (cowl tag) and the first color for the Judge per Car Distribution Bulletin 69-48.

Assembly plants

Letter in the VIN: G = Framingham, Mass.; B = Baltimore, Md.; Z = Fremont, Calif.; R = Arlington, Texas; A = Atlanta (Lakewood), Ga.

Waybill

A freight record from the trucking company showing the car, where it was picked up and the consignee it was delivered to.

Car Distribution Bulletin

A dated Pontiac Car Distribution memo to the zones announcing models, options, colors and ordering rules.
